What the service is

Ovation lets you build an invitation for an event, publish it as a link, and collect replies, photos and messages from the people you send it to. Guests do not need an account or the app to open an invitation and reply.

Your account

You need an account to create and manage your own events. Keep your sign-in details to yourself; anything done from your account is treated as done by you.

What you publish

Everything you put into an invitation stays yours. By publishing it you allow us to store it and show it to whoever holds the link, which is what publishing means here. You are responsible for having the right to use what you upload — photographs of other people included — and for the invitation not being unlawful, deceptive or abusive. We may remove content that clearly is.

Buying a plan

Publishing an invitation requires a one-off purchase for that event. It is not a subscription: nothing renews, and nothing is charged again unless you publish another event.

  • Basic — the invitation link stays live for 90 days, up to 100 guests and 50 photos.
  • Premium — the invitation link stays live for 365 days, up to 500 guests and 300 photos.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, and the price you see at the moment of purchase is the price in your own currency, set by them. When the period ends, the link stops working; the purchase covers that period and is not extended automatically.

Refunds

Because purchases go through the App Store and Google Play, refunds follow their policies and are requested from them, not from us. If something on our side went wrong, write to support@ovationapp.ch and we will help.

Availability

We try to keep invitations reachable for the whole period you paid for, but we cannot promise uninterrupted service. An invitation is not a substitute for telling your guests the details yourself if something important changes.

Ending it

You can delete an event, or your whole account, at any time from the app. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms.
