Delete your Ovation account

Ovation: Event Invitations, by Dimitrios Ligkas

You can erase your account and everything it owns at any time, from inside the app. No request, no waiting.

In the app

  1. Open Ovation and sign in.
  2. On My events, tap the account icon in the top right.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

The account is erased immediately.

If you cannot sign in

Write to support@ovationapp.ch from the address your account uses. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and sign-in identity
  • Every event you host: the invitation, its photos, schedule, people and contacts
  • Everything guests added to those events: replies, photos and guest book entries
  • Your purchase records on our side

Deletion cascades through the database in one step; nothing is kept in a recoverable state. Invitation links to your events stop working at once.

What is kept

  • Nothing personal. Anonymised aggregate counts (e.g. how many events were published in a month) contain no account data.
  • Receipts for purchases are held by Apple or Google under their own policies; we do not hold card or payment details.

Your replies as a guest

Replies, photos and wishes you left on someone else's invitation belong to that event and are not tied to an account. To remove one, ask the host — they can remove any photo or message from their event — or write to us with the invitation link.
