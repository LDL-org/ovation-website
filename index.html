What Ovation does

💌

Invitations worth sending

Wording that fits the occasion, a palette and an emblem to match, and your own photo on the cover — or a designed one until you pick it.

✅

Replies that add up

Guests say whether they are coming and with how many. You see the count, the names and the messages, without chasing anyone.

📍

Where to be, and when

Ceremony, reception, party — each with its time and place, and a tap that opens the exact spot in maps.

📷

The photos afterwards

Guests add what they took to a shared gallery, so the day comes back to one place instead of scattering across phones.

✍️

A guest book that travels

Wishes written from anywhere, including by the people who could not make it.

🔗

One link, no accounts

Guests need nothing installed and nothing signed up for. Only the person hosting keeps an account.
